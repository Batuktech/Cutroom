# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Cutroom's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces should be able to contribute without harassment.

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique behavior and code rather than people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Ask questions in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information, recordings, or private messages without permission.
- Do not post discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, threats, spam, or deliberately disruptive content.
- Respect moderation decisions and stop behavior when a maintainer asks you to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, or limit participation when needed to protect contributors. Responses should consider severity, repetition, and the people affected.

For a concern, contact a repository maintainer using a contact method they have made public, or ask them to arrange a private reporting channel without posting sensitive details. Maintainers involved in a report should not be the sole person evaluating it. Reports should be handled discreetly; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed by a public issue tracker.

This policy applies to official project spaces and to people representing Cutroom in public. It does not claim authority over unrelated private activity.
