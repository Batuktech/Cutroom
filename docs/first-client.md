# Turn a recording into a deliverable

Cutroom gives you a concrete service you can demonstrate: turn a client's existing interview, tutorial, or presentation into short captioned clips. It helps with production; finding a client and agreeing a paid scope still require your judgment and conversation.

## Start with one small package

A clear first offer is **three clips from one recording**, each with an agreed duration, aspect ratio, reviewed captions, an MP4, and an editable SRT. Agree the source length, language, delivery date, number of revisions, and who approves the final cuts before starting. Define your own price from the work involved and what the client agrees to; the app does not set a market rate.

Use footage you own or that the client authorizes you to edit. For your first demonstration, make a short recording of yourself explaining one useful topic, then produce one landscape and one vertical version. The bundled sample teaches the controls, but a real person on camera makes a more relevant portfolio example.

## A repeatable production session

1. Import the recording and rename the project for the client and recording date.
2. Transcribe locally. Correct names, technical terms, and any uncertain lines while listening to the original audio.
3. Find a passage that makes sense by itself. Check the opening, context, and ending. Suggestions are starting points for your edit decisions.
4. Duplicate a clip to make another aspect ratio. Adjust the framing separately and keep important faces and text inside the visible frame.
5. Preview captions on a small screen. The app groups long text into short cues automatically. Correct the transcript and adjust caption size when a line is difficult to read; check timing after text changes. To change subtitle segment boundaries, edit the SRT in a text editor and import it again.
6. Export, then watch the rendered MP4 from beginning to end with sound. Check the first and last half-second, caption accuracy, framing, and any black bars you intentionally chose.
7. Download the delivery ZIP. Keep a copy of the metadata and source footage while the revision window is open.

## Before handing over

- Each clip communicates a complete idea and ends cleanly.
- The agreed aspect ratio and duration are correct.
- Names, spelling, and subtitle timing have been reviewed.
- The actual exported video plays with sound and readable captions.
- The delivery pack contains the versions you intend to deliver. It defaults to the latest render per clip; include older versions only when you intend to hand those over.
- The client knows whether captions are burned in, and receives the editable SRT as agreed.
